# [[h15-beast|H15 Beast]] Server Setup & Hardware Upgrades

Basic overview of what I did.

Working on the [[h15-beast|H15 Beast server]] and was able to source 2x32 GB of RAM on Facebook marketplace for $650 (due to the insane RAM prices and shortage going on).

Got all the drives installed and attempting to get proxmox installed.

Debugging issues related to the HBA Broadcom 9400-16i not showing up. Also found out I need the 24i variant since the 6-bay ssd cage has 2 of the Mini SAS ports.

Was not able to get proxmox installed but was able to update the BIOS and access remotely over the iKVM.
