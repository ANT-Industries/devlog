Troubleshooting the HBA Broadcom 9400-16i & SAS Upgrades

On this day I was able to troubleshoot the 9400-16i and found out that I have a broken or clone card which was white I got green flashing lights after post, but nothing i could do would get it to show up in BIOS (even EFI shell).

I ordered a 9305-24i from "The Art of the Server" on eBay and should be a much better card.

Turns out there are a lot of clones on the market coming from China and there is a huge price gap under $200 and over $500. My 9400-16i was $135 which I though was the correct price for used.
